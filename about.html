Who we are

Creative thinking.
Production focus.

AEDMedia is a Los Angeles-based entertainment and live-event production company focused on hosting, producing and developing memorable shows.

We bring creative ideas and practical coordination together — helping artists, organizers, promoters and partners move from concept to showtime. Our work is guided by collaboration, clear communication and respect for the audience experience.

Our journey

Rooted in live
culture. Always evolving.

AEDMedia's story has been shaped by a range of live entertainment settings, creative collaborations and event experiences.

Over time, that perspective has grown across music-led gatherings, stage productions, audience experiences and special events. Each opportunity brings a different mix of talent, partners and production needs — and each one adds to the way we approach the next.

We keep the focus on the work, the people behind it and the moments shared with the audience. Our history is best understood as a continuing journey through live entertainment, rather than a list of names.

Our point of view

Every great show has a story. We help bring it to the stage.

From early planning to the energy in the room, the details matter. We aim to make the experience feel effortless for the audience and well-coordinated for the people creating it.
